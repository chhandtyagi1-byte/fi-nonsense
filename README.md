# fi-nonsense 🦖🪙
> *"track your monthly expenses so you dont have to starve at the month end"*

A vibrant retro 8-bit pixel game finance tracker for **Indian Rupees (₹)** featuring **Brokezilla**, your savage, fire-breathing, hilariously judgmental dinosaur financial advisor.

---

## 🎮 What's New & Upgraded

1. **Ultra-Vibrant Punchy Colors**:
   - Bright retro arcade sunshine yellow (`#FFE600`), electric coral (`#FF2A6D`), emerald pixel green (`#00E676`), and arcade cyan (`#00F0FF`).
   - Gentle CRT scanline overlay (calibrated to low opacity so colors stay bright and pop!).
2. **Big Indian Rupee Coins & Currency Notes (Interactive Background)**:
   - **Large Chunky Coins**: ₹10, ₹20 bimetallic coins and ₹5 brass coins with detailed rupee symbols (`₹`), denominations, and glints.
   - **Large Currency Notes**: Authentic colorful notes in ₹500 (stone-grey/olive green), ₹200 (bright saffron orange), ₹100 (lavender violet), ₹50 (teal cyan), and ₹2000 (magenta pink) with Reserve Bank of India typography.
3. **Dedicated Physical Sound Effects**:
   - **Metallic Clink**: Pure ringing metal chime when you grab, toss, or collide Rupee coins.
   - **Paper Rustle**: Crisp currency paper flutter when you pick up or fling Rupee notes.
4. **Complete Indian Rupees (₹) Overhaul**:
   - All budgets, calculations, caps, and charts formatted in **₹ (INR)** with Indian numbering format (e.g. `₹25,000`, `₹1,200/day`).
   - Presets for Indian living: PG / Rent (`₹8,000`), WiFi (`₹799`), Gym (`₹1,500`), OTT / Subs (`₹499`).
   - Brokezilla dialogue and habit recognition tailored for Indian college & lifestyle habits:
     - Daily Bisleri water bottle: ₹20/day (₹600/month)
     - Cutting chai & cold coffee: ₹50 - ₹150
     - Weekend friends hangout: ₹1,500 - ₹2,500
     - Swiggy & Zomato delivery surcharge roasts!
